Appendix M — Pair-Level Statistical Robustness (Task 2)

Status. Post-freeze statistical addendum. No frozen value is modified; this appendix quantifies how the Task 2 chain and joint pass rates behave when uncertainty is quantified at the correct level of the design.

M.0 What is and is not a replicate here

The 4 runs per system are technical repeats of a single administration protocol (temperature 0, prompts byte-identical, serving through relay endpoints is not bit-deterministic). They quantify serving-level run-to-run variability and are pooled as such in the main tables. They are not independent biological replicates, and nowhere in this appendix are runs resampled as if they were independent experiments. The resampling unit is the TF–gene pair: all units and all runs belonging to a pair move together in every bootstrap replicate. Task 2 contains 168 pairs over 4632 unit-runs (6 systems × 4 runs).

M.1 Bootstrap confidence intervals over the 168 pairs

Estimator per system: the unweighted mean over pairs of the per-pair pass rate (a pair's rate averages its 1–2 units × 4 runs). Pair-weighted points sit 2–4.5 pp above the published unit-run-pooled values because the latter weight the multi-unit pairs (and the 99-pair ZNF143 collection) by their unit-run counts. M.4 Study-level bootstrap

Pairs were mapped to their primary source study from the evaluator-only provenance inventory (GSE accession, else DOI; rule: primary = lexicographically first identifier among the pair's sources (5 pairs have 2 sources)). This yields 31 studies over 168 pairs — dominated by GSE266489 (99 pairs); GSE45822 (10 pairs); GSE274688 (7 pairs).

Concentration disclosure. One study (GSE266489, the ZNF143 collection) contributes 99 of 168 pairs (58.9%). Study-level resampling therefore has a small effective sample: the CIs below are wide, asymmetric, and mostly reflect the uncertainty of that single collection. They are reported for completeness, not as the primary uncertainty statement.

Interpretation: the benchmark's pair diversity — and therefore its strongest current evidence — is concentrated in one large, internally homogeneous collection of easy (direct-cis, ZNF143) pairs. This is the same concentration documented from the label side in Appendix D.6 (ZNF143 = 99/193 units; chain pass 97.0% on ZNF143 vs 55.5% elsewhere). Future benchmark versions should rebalance toward more independent studies.

M.5 Summary

(i) All per-system pass rates carry ≈±5 pp pair-level uncertainty; claims should not be made at finer resolution. (ii) The two tier boundaries — GPT-6-Astra above the field, GLM-5.3-Flash below it — are robust; the four-system middle tier is not ordered by this benchmark. (iii) Study-level uncertainty is dominated by a single source collection and is disclosed rather than hidden. (iv) The four runs per system quantify protocol repeatability only and are never treated as biological replicates.
