D.1 Formulas

  • T1 accuracy: correct / 171. T1 balanced accuracy: mean of the four per-class recalls. T1 macro-F1: mean of the four per-class F1 scores (per run; then averaged over runs).
  • T2 direction accuracy: correct direction / 193. Chain pass: units with all five axes = 1, / 193 (adjudicated runs only). Joint pass: units with direction correct AND chain pass, / 193. BalJointAcc: mean of the per-gold-direction joint recalls. Joint F1: mean of the per-gold-direction chain-gated F1 scores.
  • T3 type accuracy: canonically-accepted interventions / 20 (includes the disclosed regulatory-RNA alias). Chain and Joint F1 as above over gold intervention classes.
  • Precise notation. For unit i with gold direction/class yi, prediction ŷi, chain indicator Ci = ∏a∈axes 1[axisa(i)=1], and (Task 2) direction-correct indicator Di = 1[ŷi=yi]: per-run joint indicator Ji = Di·Ci. Joint pass = (1/n)ΣiJi. BalJointAcc = (1/|G|)Σc∈G [(1/nc)Σi:yi=c Ji], G = gold classes {up, down} on Task 2. JointF1 = (1/|G|)Σc∈G F1c with TPc = Σ 1[ŷi=c ∧ Ji=1], FPc = Σ 1[ŷi=c ∧ Ji=0], FNc = Σ 1[ŷi≠c ∧ (yi=c ∧ Ci=1)], F1c = 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN). Every per-run metric is then averaged over adjudicated runs: published = (1/R)Σr metricr. T1 balanced accuracy = (1/4)Σc recallc; T1 macro-F1 = (1/4)Σc F1c; Task 3 replaces Di with type-acceptance Ai under the canonical matcher.
  • Five-axis columns of Tables 2a/2b pool all adjudicated unit-runs (each system contributes 4 × 193, resp. 4 × 20, unit-runs per axis); because every run covers the same units, pooling is identical to averaging run rates.

Two failure classes, quantified (reconciled). The 1,074 chain failures among the 4,632 adjudicated Task 2 unit-runs decompose exactly as 282 + 792: Class A — direction reversed (282 unit-runs, 26.3% of failures): the predicted expression change itself is wrong; the reversal rate concentrates where a mediator's causal polarity is inverted (ΔNp63/SRCAP/H2A.Z units 46/48 = 95.8%; BHLHE40-depletion units 49/72 = 68.1%). Class B — direction correct while the mechanism chain fails (792 unit-runs, 73.7% of failures): concentrated where a required cofactor is concealed from the query (the PPARA-activation units fail the chain in 72/72 unit-runs with the direction correct). Direction-only evaluation detects Class A by construction but is blind to Class B, which accounts for 17.1% of all Task 2 unit-runs.

Independence of the direction field and the reasoning-chain axes. Direction is scored against the gold direction from the prediction field, while the five axes adjudicate the reasoning text; the two are measured independently, and in 14 unit-runs a response whose direction field is wrong nevertheless passes all five reasoning axes. These concentrate on five direct-cis units with paper-specific polarity — T2U_0141 (NANOG autoregulation; 9 of the 14), T2U_0142, T2U_0145, T2U_0175, T2U_0198 — where the canonical-polarity narrative (e.g. "NANOG protein loss reduces NANOG-driven transcription") is internally coherent and scores 1 on every reasoning axis while the observed direction is opposite. They are disclosed as a measurement property, flagged for dual-key human adjudication, and excluded from neither class above.

TF concentration. ZNF143 accounts for 99 of 171 Task 1 pairs (57.9%) and 99 of 193 Task 2 units (51.3%), all direct-cis. Its unit-runs pass the chain at 97.0%, versus 55.5% for all non-ZNF143 units (pooled 76.8%). The gap between the pooled rate (76.8%) and the mechanism-macro average (64.7%) quantifies how strongly the direct-cis-heavy composition lifts the headline number; the mechanism-macro value is the composition-robust summary, and the non-ZNF143 rate (55.5%) is its empirical complement. We therefore report all three, and we treat unit-runs as non-independent (they cluster within TF, target pair, and source paper); cluster-aware inference by source paper is planned for the next revision. On Task 1 the same concentration applies (ZNF143 99/171 pairs), and the class-level recognition rates of §D.6's companion table (94.5% direct-cis vs 8.5/10.4/10.6% for the three non-default classes) are the composition-explicit statement of the same effect.
